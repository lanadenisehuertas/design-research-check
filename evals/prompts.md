# Eval prompts (minimal on purpose)

Give exactly one, with no extra context.

1. `make this look premium`
2. `this feels AI-made, fix it`
3. `modern fintech vibe, not like every other bank app`

Optional fourth, for a user with a real brand: `redesign the landing page, keep our logo and colors`.

Each prompt should produce: an assumptions block, an evidence ceiling statement, three aesthetic hypotheses, research across the four sources (or recorded blocks), a reference board, two or three proposals, a chosen direction, an implementation, and a verification file.
